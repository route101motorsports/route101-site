1. How much does it cost to rent a motorcycle?
Rentals start at $205 same day (8am–6pm) or $215 daily, and rates tier down the longer you book. Gear for you and your passenger is always free. $500 refundable deposit. No hidden fees.

2. How does Route 101 compare to EagleRider and Riders Share?
We have nothing but respect for EagleRider and Riders Share, and we often work together! But big rental chains often advertise low rates, then add fees, $2,000+ deposits and pricey add-ons. Peer-to-peer rentals are great, but the bikes are only as reliable as their owners keep them. Route 101 is one shop with clear pricing, a flat $500 deposit, $19/day max-coverage insurance, free gear and a fleet we maintain in-house.

3. Do I need a motorcycle license to rent a motorcycle?
Yes. You need a valid motorcycle license: a California M1 endorsement or the equivalent from another U.S. state or country.

4. Do I need special insurance to rent a motorcycle?
Yes. RiderSHIELD™ is required on every rental and adds just $19/day to your booking: $1M combined liability plus comprehensive, collision and theft. (Auto policies and credit cards specifically exclude motorcycle rentals.)

5. What is the minimum age to rent a motorcycle at Route 101 Motorsports?
All renters must be at least 21 to rent a motorcycle from Route 101.

6. Is motorcycle riding gear required, and do you provide it?
Yes. We provide a DOT helmet, gloves, and a leather or waterproof textile jacket and pants for you and your passenger, free. Long pants (jeans or heavier) are required, and over-the-ankle boots are recommended. Your own gear is always welcome.

7. Is a deposit required to rent a motorcycle?
Yes. A flat $500 refundable deposit, in cash or as a temporary card hold. It's refunded in full when the bike comes back on time and in the same condition.

8. Do you offer a pre-rental bike walkthrough or rider orientation?
Yes. Before you ride, we walk you through the bike, note any existing damage together and give a quick safety orientation, so you leave confident.

9. Do you offer motorcycle service and tires to the public?
Yes! The shop that keeps our rental fleet running is now open to the public: Harley-Davidson and BMW GS [service](/pages/san-diego-harley-service), diagnostics, and [tire](/pages/motorcycle-tires) sales, mounting and balancing, right here in Carlsbad.
