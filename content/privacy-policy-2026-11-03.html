1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

Route 101 Motorsports, LLC ("Route 101," "we," "us" or "our") rents motorcycles and services motorcycles in Carlsbad, California. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and share information when you:

  • visit or use our website and online booking pages (the "Platform");
  • rent a motorcycle, book a service or buy gear from us;
  • call, text or email us; or
  • otherwise give us Personal Information (together, the "Services").

By using the Platform or the Services, or by giving us Personal Information, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Changes take effect when we post them here. If you keep using the Services after a change, you accept the updated policy.

3. What "Personal Information" Means

  1. Personal Information is information that identifies you, such as your name, address, phone number, email address, social media account or other account identifier.
  2. It also includes details such as your date of birth, gender, general location and preferences when they are linked to information that identifies you.
  3. It does not include aggregate information: data about how groups of people use our Services, with everything that identifies an individual removed.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Information you give us

  • Contact details, such as your name, phone number, email address and mailing address.
  • Rental and booking details, such as your dates, the motorcycle and add-ons you choose, and the information we need to confirm you are eligible to ride, such as your driver's license and motorcycle endorsement.
  • Service details, such as your motorcycle's year, make, model and mileage, and the work you request.
  • Messages you send us through our forms, by email, by text or by phone, and any reviews you post.

4.2 Payment information

  • Card and payment details are collected and processed by our third-party payment provider, not stored by us.
  • That provider may be located outside your country. Its own privacy policy governs how it handles your payment information, and we are not responsible for its practices.

4.3 Information collected automatically

  • When you use the Platform, we and the companies we work with may use cookies, pixel tags, local storage and similar technologies (together, "Cookies").
  • We use Cookies to keep the site working, remember your preferences, understand how the site is used, measure our advertising, keep accounts secure and help prevent fraud.

4.4 Information from others

  • Where permitted by law, we may receive information from identity verification services, insurance and claims providers, and similar sources.
  • If you sign in through a third-party account (for example, Google or Facebook), that service may share information with us. What it shares is controlled by that service and its privacy policy.

4.5 Phone calls

  • We may record calls for quality, documentation, record keeping and training.
  • If you do not want your call recorded, please email us at info@route101motorsports.com instead.

4.6 Information about other people

  • If you give us information about someone else, such as a passenger or a second rider, you confirm that you have their permission to do so.

5. How We Use Your Information

  1. To provide the Services: process bookings, rentals, service work and payments, and send confirmations and receipts.
  2. To verify your identity and eligibility to rent, including license checks.
  3. To give customer support and answer your questions.
  4. To contact you by phone, text or email about your booking, rental, service or account.
  5. To send promotions and offers, if you have agreed to receive them (see Sections 6 and 7).
  6. To prevent, detect and investigate fraud, damage, theft and other harmful or illegal activity.
  7. To resolve disputes, collect amounts owed, handle insurance and damage claims, and enforce our Terms of Service and rental agreements.
  8. To improve our website, Services and advertising.
  9. To run referral programs, discounts, surveys and promotions.
  10. To comply with the law, and for any other purpose we tell you about and you agree to.

6. Text Messaging (SMS)

6.1 What we collect. If you opt in to texts from Route 101 Motorsports, we collect your mobile number, the record of your consent and your message history with us.

6.2 How you opt in. You may opt in:

  • verbally, by telling us during a call or in person that we may text you; or
  • by checking the text-message consent box on a form on our website.

Consent to receive texts is not required to rent, book service or buy anything from us.

6.3 What we send.

  • Booking and rental confirmations and reminders.
  • Customer support replies.
  • Motorcycle service updates and service completion notices.
  • Deals and updates, only if you checked the marketing consent box. Marketing texts are limited to no more than 4 per month.

6.4 How often. Message frequency varies with your bookings and service visits.

6.5 Cost. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier and plan.

6.6 Opting out. Reply STOP to any text at any time. We will send one message confirming you are unsubscribed, and then you will receive no more texts from us. You can also ask to be removed by calling (888) 509-5032 or emailing info@route101motorsports.com.

6.7 Help. Reply HELP to any text, call (888) 509-5032 or email info@route101motorsports.com.

6.8 No sharing of mobile information. We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number, text-message consent or opt-in information with any third party or affiliate for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.

7. Email

  • We email you about your bookings, rentals, service and account.
  • If you sign up for our mailing list, we also send occasional deals and news. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

8. Cookies and Advertising

  • We use analytics and advertising partners, such as Google, to understand how our website is used and to show our ads on other websites.
  • These partners may set or read Cookies on your browser, including through pixel tags.
  • You can set your browser to block or delete Cookies, and you can change your cookie choices with the cookie preferences link at the bottom of this page. If you block Cookies, parts of the website may not work properly.

We do not sell your Personal Information. We share it only as described below.

  3. Legal and safety reasons: to comply with the law or legal process; to respond to lawful requests from government authorities; to enforce our Terms of Service, rental agreements and this policy; and to protect the rights, property and safety of Route 101, our customers and others.
