## Tires we sell and install
- Brands: Michelin, Dunlop, Metzeler, Pirelli, Bridgestone, Shinko, Maxxis, Mitas. Other brands: ask in the quote request.
- Street, touring, sport, adventure (50/50, 70/30 and similar) and MX/dirt tires. Mounted and balanced in-house in Carlsbad.
- Same-day install if the tire is in stock. Most installs are done while you wait, by appointment.
- Typical time: about 1 hour for one tire, about 2.5 hours for both.
- Drop-off from 8am, pick-up by 6pm.
- Nothing to prep or bring. Trailer parking available (truck and trailer OK).
- Spoke wheels with tubes, TUbliss and rim locks: yes. Tell us if you need new tubes.
- MX/dirt tires generally aren't balanced; ask a tire tech when you come in.

## Tires bought elsewhere (online or another store)
- Happy to install them.
- Bring just the wheels: from $60 per wheel, mounted and balanced.
- Bring the bike: removal, reinstall, mount and balance from $100.
- Both are confirmed case by case in the quote.

## Not offered
- No trikes, sidecars, scooters, e-motos (like Sur-Ron), ATVs/UTVs, golf carts, pit bikes or minibikes.
- No flat repairs, patches or plugs.
- No towing, pickup or roadside assistance.
- Vintage bikes, 21"/23" custom fronts, whitewalls and sand/dune paddles: call to check.

## Quotes
- Send the tire quote form (or let the Tire Advisor send it for you).
- Quotes are emailed, usually within 20 to 30 minutes during business hours.
- A quote is good for 7 days.
- Mount and balance is a flat fee that varies slightly by bike; it's itemized in the quote.

## Fees and extras
- Valve stems and TPMS sensors are not included. If needed, we'll tell you and quote them at install.
- California tire fees: $1.75 per tire plus a $3.75 per tire CA recycling fee. Take your old tires home and you avoid the $3.75 recycling fee.
- Road hazard warranty available: about $25 per tire.
- Every brand carries the manufacturer's warranty against defects; terms vary by brand, so ask about the brand you choose.

## Paying and saving
- Nothing is due until the work is done.
- We accept all major credit cards, Venmo, Zelle and Cash App.
- Price match: we'll try to match a verified price from a local tire dealer or dealership. We don't price match online retailers.
- No cash discount.
- 10% military discount with valid military ID; mention it in the quote request.

## Shipping
- We can ship tires to you. Orders over $200 ship free; under $200 adds shipping. Call or send a request for the shipping cost.

## Loaner bikes
- Happy to put you on one of our rental bikes while yours is being worked on. Ask your tire tech about pricing.

## Rental fleet tires (keep it general)
- Our fleet runs several brands and models chosen for high use and high mileage. The best tire for you is the one that fits your bike, your model and how you ride.
