# Route 101 Motorsports: Advisor knowledge (STARTER, to be expanded with Glenn and G)

## Business basics
- Route 101 Motorsports, 6351 Corte Del Abeto #107, Carlsbad, CA 92011. Phone (888) 509-5032.
- Open 7 days, 8am to 6pm, by appointment only. No walk-ins.
- Route 101 Garage does service and tires. 100% 5-star reviews.
- Rider Lounge: grab a free Red Bull or coffee and relax, or ask about a loaner bike.

## Tires
- Brands we carry: Michelin, Dunlop, Metzeler, Pirelli, Bridgestone, Shinko, Maxxis, Mitas.
- Street, adventure and MX (dirt bike) tires. Mounted and balanced in-house in Carlsbad.
- Most installs are done while you wait, by appointment.
- We confirm your price before anything is ordered.
- For options and prices: send the tire quote form on the tire page with year, make and model, front, rear or both, and your riding style. We reply with a list of options and prices to choose from.
- We do NOT mount pit bike or minibike tires. We do NOT do flat repairs, patches or plugs.
